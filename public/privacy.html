Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

BandWagon helps bandmates suggest songs, vote, and agree on a playlist. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Who operates BandWagon

BandWagon at bandwagon-it23.onrender.com is operated by Charles Monroe. For privacy questions or requests about your information, email charlesmonroeofficial@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: your Google account identifier, name, and email address. We use these to create your account and recognize you when you sign in.
  • Band activity: bands you create or join, membership, invitation links, song titles and artists, YouTube video identifiers, your notes, reactions, objection explanations, and setlist approvals.
  • Sign-in records: session identifiers and expiry times, and temporary values used to protect the Google sign-in process. Session identifiers are stored hashed in the database.
  • Technical information: our hosting providers may process IP addresses, request information, and operational logs needed to deliver and protect the service.

BandWagon does not ask for your Google password or access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or calendars. Google access tokens are used during sign-in to retrieve your account information and are not stored in BandWagon’s database.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, maintain your band memberships, enforce voting rules, display suggestions and reactions, build setlists, and operate and troubleshoot the app. We do not sell your personal information or use Google account information for advertising.

BandWagon’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What your bandmates can see

Members of the same band can see your display name, suggestions, votes, notes, objection explanations, and setlist approvals. Voting is not anonymous. Your email address is not included in the band member list. Anyone with a valid band invitation link can join after signing in, so share invitations only with people you want in the band. The owner can replace the invitation link.

Service providers and YouTube

BandWagon uses Render to host the app and Neon to store app data. Google provides account sign-in.

If you choose to play a song, BandWagon loads an embedded YouTube player using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain. YouTube may then receive technical information and process playback activity or cookies under its own policies. Opening songs or a setlist on YouTube takes you to that service. BandWagon does not upload your band’s votes or notes to YouTube.

These providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Database hosting is configured in the region selected by the operator; other provider processing locations depend on their services.

Cookies and browser storage

BandWagon uses an essential sign-in cookie that expires after 30 days, and a temporary Google sign-in cookie that expires after 10 minutes. Signing out removes the current app session. The app uses browser session storage to remember an invitation while you sign in. It does not include advertising or analytics tracking scripts.

The separate browser-local demo saves its example data in your browser. Clearing that site’s storage removes that demo data. Demo bands in the full app are stored in the app database and use simulated members.

Retention and your choices

Account and band records remain available until removed by the operator. Expired sign-in records may remain in the database until cleaned up; their expiry prevents them from being used to sign in. Hosting logs and database backups follow the relevant provider’s retention settings.

You can change or remove your own reactions and suggestions while voting is open. To request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact charlesmonroeofficial@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the account is yours before acting. Removing participation records may change a shared band’s voting totals and approvals. Deleted records may remain in provider backups until those backups expire.

You can also remove BandWagon’s access through your Google account’s third-party connections. Revoking Google access does not by itself delete existing BandWagon records or end an existing BandWagon session; sign out and contact us to request deletion.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and revise the date above. For questions or requests, contact Charles Monroe at charlesmonroeofficial@gmail.com.
